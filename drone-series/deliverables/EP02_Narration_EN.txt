A propeller engine spins a propeller that pushes air backwards.
A jet engine skips the propeller and throws air backwards directly, and much faster.
The simplest jet engine is called a turbojet, and it does four things in order.
First, a fan-like compressor at the front squeezes the incoming air hard.
Second, fuel is sprayed into that compressed air and burned continuously.
The gas gets extremely hot and expands.
Third, that hot gas spins another fan at the back, the turbine.
The turbine sits on the same shaft as the compressor, so the engine keeps itself turning.
Fourth, whatever energy is left blasts out of the back at high speed.
It's like blowing up a balloon and letting it go: the air rushes out, and the balloon shoots forward.
Push gas backwards, and the recoil pushes the aircraft forward.
Small jet engines like this have been used for decades in target drones and some cruise missiles.
